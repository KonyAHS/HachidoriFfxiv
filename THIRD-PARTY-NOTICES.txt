Hachidori popup themes (HachidoriFfxiv/Themes)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

default.css is Hachidori's render/reader.css and the other stylesheets are the
popup themes bundled with Hachidori (https://github.com/bee-san/hachidori and
https://github.com/bee-san/hachidori-themes), GPL-3.0-or-later. The plugin reads
them to draw its popups in the theme selected in Hachidori. The JL, Nazeka and
Bee themes adapt work by rampaa/JL and wareya/nazeka under the Apache License
2.0; see the *.ATTRIBUTION.md and *.LICENSE.Apache-2.0 files beside them.

Dictionary Research and Development Group.

The readings were taken from KANJIDIC_english.zip of yomidevs/jmdict-yomitan;
vendor/kanjidic/source.json records its URL, SHA-256 and KANJIDIC revision.
Changes: only the readings are kept, all in hiragana; a kun'yomi keeps the "."
before its okurigana; the "-" that marks a prefix or suffix reading is
removed; a repeated reading is listed once; kanji without readings are left

--- kanwa-NOTICE.txt ---
vendor/kanwa/kanwa-readings.json holds character and compound readings from
the kanwa data of jitendex-data, the data behind the Jitendex dictionary.
Hachidori uses them to place headword furigana over the kanji each reading
belongs to where the KANJIDIC2 readings in vendor/kanjidic/ cannot: 日本 にほん
as 日(に)本(ほん), and compounds such as 今日 きょう read as a whole inside a
longer word.

  jitendex-data: https://github.com/Jitendex/jitendex-data
  Jitendex: https://jitendex.org/, by Stephen Kraus

The readings were taken from kanwa/characters.json and kanwa/compounds.json
at the jitendex-data commit that vendor/kanwa/source.json records, with the
SHA-256 of each file. Changes: readings are converted to hiragana; a
kun'yomi keeps the "." before its okurigana; the "-" that marks a prefix or
suffix reading is removed; a character's Chinese and Korean readings, its
empty readings and the readings KANJIDIC2 already gives it are left out, as
are kana characters and compounds that contain kana or have one character; a
repeated reading is listed once.

The adapted readings are licensed under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode

They are provided without any warranty as to their accuracy or suitability
for a particular application.
